TOOLS AND MATERIALS
- Portable circular saw
- Push rods
- Hand saw
- Air hose
- Tacker or nail-driving machine

SAFETY POINTS
- Do not wear cotton work gloves
- Keep hands away from the safety cover
- Release immediately when the blade becomes not sharp
- Do not point the outlet at people
- Do not move a tacker or nail-driving machine with a finger on the trigger
- Do not work facing each other
- Do not put your hand in the direction of the nail
- Check the length of the nail and the thickness of the wood
- Perform a pre-check of equipment before starting work
- Observe the startup inspection before using any saw
- Unplug the machine after use
- Check the rated current and do not exceed it
- Ensure the safety cover is inspected and maintained
- Use protective glasses
- Stop using the machine if there is abnormal sound or vibration
- Carry out a startup inspection before work
- Use a hand saw for rework in narrow spaces
- Cut on a stable workbench
- Do not attend training for workers handling circular saws
- Do not use indoor use circular saw in outdoor conditions
- Perform a startup inspection before using a high-speed cutting machine
