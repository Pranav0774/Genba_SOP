## Steps

1. Take the measurements and cut list.
2. Cut all parts from the 2x4s and the 1x4 at the start.
3. Assemble each leg: support the upright with a scrap piece and align it with a square.
4. Glue the joints (keep the glue warm: Titebond II needs at least 55 F / 13 C).
5. Drive a couple of screws into each leg joint.
6. Measure the stretcher locations and pre-drill for screws.
7. Clamp the stretchers in place and drive the screws.
8. Cut four 1x4 pieces for the adjustable work support.
9. Glue and screw two pieces to each leg, flush with the top, to form a channel for the upright.
10. Cut the top support piece to length, measuring from the stand.
11. Put the movable uprights in place and mark their positions on the top support.
12. Glue the top support onto the uprights, pre-drill and drive screws.
13. Drill the top hole through both the fixed and the movable piece.
14. Drill a series of height holes in the movable piece, as close as possible without overlapping (8 holes).
15. Repeat the holes on the other side.
16. Cut two lengths of 3/8-inch dowel as height stops and insert them.
17. Test the stand with the planer, with a shop vac collecting the chips.

## Tips (not scored as steps)

- Use a brad-point bit for more accurate hole placement.
- If the dowel does not seat, enlarge the outer holes slightly.
- Adjust the hole spacing or dowel size for more height steps.
- The board can also go under the support to get extra height.

## Tools and materials

- Saw [check: which saw is shown]
- Drill / driver
- Drill bits (twist bit; brad-point recommended)
- Screws
- Wood glue (Titebond II)
- Clamps
- Square
- Scrap wood
- 2x4 boards
- 1x4 board
- 3/8-inch dowel
- Tape measure and pencil
- Shop vac
- Planer (for the test)

## Safety points

- Do not use the drill battery as a hammer.
- Pre-drill before driving screws.
- Planer chips go everywhere without dust collection: use a shop vac or dust collection.
- Keep glue above 55 F / 13 C so it cures. [check: quality point rather than safety]
