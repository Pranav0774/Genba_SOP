## Steps

### Set up the machine
1. [00:00] Plug the power cord and the foot pedal into the side of the machine.
2. [00:00] Turn the machine on.
3. [00:00] Check that a needle is installed; spare needles, bobbins and tools are in the accessory compartment.
4. [00:00] Choose a needle size for the fabric: 70/10 for silk, 80/10 for everyday projects, 90/14 for denim.
5. [01:20] Install or replace the needle: flat side facing the back, push it up to the top, screw it in place.
6. [01:20] To remove the presser foot, press the lever behind the foot to release it.
7. [01:20] To attach a presser foot, slide it underneath and push the bar up until it clicks.
8. [01:20] Raise or lower the presser foot with the lever at the back when you start and finish sewing.

### Wind the bobbin
9. [01:20] Read the manual for your machine's threading system.
10. [02:25] Place the thread spool on the spool pin.
11. [02:25] Put an empty bobbin on the bobbin winder and enable winding (slide the winder over, or pull out the hand wheel, depending on the machine).
12. [02:25] Bring the thread from the spool around the guide (follow the arrow) to the bobbin and wrap it a few times.
13. [03:39] Press the foot pedal to wind the bobbin until it is full.
14. [03:39] Switch the winder back off and cut the bobbin thread from the spool.

### Thread the machine
15. [03:39] Place the bobbin in the bobbin case and pull out a little thread.
16. [03:39] Thread the top thread following the arrows, catching the take-up hook (turn the hand wheel if the hook is not visible).
17. [04:55] Bring the thread down to the needle and behind the small thread guide.
18. [04:55] Thread the needle by hand or with the automatic needle threader.
19. [04:55] Hold the top thread with the left hand and turn the hand wheel towards you, down and back up, to pull up the bobbin thread.
20. [04:55] Pull both threads to the back of the machine.

### Prepare the fabric
21. [06:01] Place the two fabric layers right sides together.
22. [06:01] Add seam allowance (about 1/2 inch) when cutting the fabric.
23. [07:28] Pin or clip the layers together.
24. [07:28] Mark the seam line with a temporary marker (chalk, disappearing-ink pen or soap) that will not stain.

### Sew
25. [07:28] Raise the presser foot, slide the fabric under it and line it up with the seam allowance.
26. [08:42] Set the stitch: straight stitch by default, longest length for basting, 3 to 3.5 for topstitching.
27. [08:42] Backstitch at the start: sew a few stitches forward, then hold the backstitch button for a few stitches back.
28. [08:42] Sew the rest of the seam forward.
29. [06:01] At a corner, sew to the corner, raise the presser foot, turn the fabric and continue.
30. [08:42] Backstitch at the end of the seam.

## Tips (not scored as steps)

- Buy extra bobbins compatible with the machine for different thread colours.
- Instead of marking the fabric, put tape or a rubber band on the machine at the seam allowance as a guide.
- Measure from the needle with a ruler to check where the seam allowance is.
- Do not backstitch a basting (temporary) stitch.
- Cotton fabric is easiest for beginner projects.

## Tools and materials

- Sewing machine
- Power cord and foot pedal
- Needles (Schmetz Universal 70/10, 80/10, 90/14)
- Presser feet
- Thread (100% polyester, Gutermann)
- Bobbins
- Fabric scissors
- Rotary cutter and cutting mat (optional)
- Clear straight ruler
- Tape measure
- Seam ripper
- Fabric (100% cotton)
- Pins or clips
- Chalk, disappearing-ink pen or bar of soap
- Tape or rubber band (seam guide)
- Machine manual
