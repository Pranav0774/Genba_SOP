# eng_05 reference process: Coca-Cola bottling and canning

Status: VERIFIED (checked against the video by Pranav; same as E:\SOPs_Hand_Written\eng_05 Hand - Written SOP.docx)

Note: documentary narration over stock footage, no worker performing a task. This key lists
process stages, not worker steps. The ground truth starts mid-sentence ("However, water is...").

## Process stages

1. Source water from on-site wells, sanitise it and pass it through nanofiltration.
2. Deliver the syrup to the plant in plastic jugs by forklift.
3. Dilute the syrup with water in the mixing unit.
4. Add sweetener (high-fructose corn syrup for classic Coke, aspartame for Diet Coke and Coke Zero).
5. Blend the mixture and pump it through pipes into batch tanks.
6. Carbonate the mixture with CO2 in the carbonator.
7. Rinse the bottles: flip them upside down, flush with filtered water, turn them upright.
8. Label the bottles with the hot-melt labelling machine.
9. Inspect the bottles for cracks or bad seals and remove failures.
10. Fill the bottles with the mixture kept at 41 to 43 degrees, removing air first.
11. Cap the bottles (caps fed down an air chute and twisted on); crimp aluminium crowns on glass bottles.
12. Warm the bottles to room temperature so they do not sweat in packaging.
13. Inspect the bottles a final time for impurities and put them into cases.
14. Clean the cans in the can-cleaning system.
15. Fill the cans with the mixture.
16. Seal the cans in the can seamer (a worker keeps it supplied with lids).
17. Wash the cans and inspect them with cameras and sensors for bad seams or spills.
18. Wrap the bottles in cardboard (cut from a reel, shaped, held with clips), then shrink-wrap them.
19. Pack the cans into boxes and shrink-wrap them.
20. Sort and stack the cases automatically.
21. Shrink-wrap the sorted cases for stability.
22. Stack the cases onto pallets with robots.
23. Load the pallets onto trucks.
