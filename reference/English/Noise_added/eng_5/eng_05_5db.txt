 Water is approximately 90% of the formula.
 At the plant, water is sourced from on-site wells, thoroughly sanitized, and put through
 a nanofiltration process to ensure absolute purity, providing the perfect base for the
 beverage.
 This meticulous water treatment guarantees that every bottle of Coke delivers the same
 crisp, refreshing taste.
 Now, with the secret formula in hand, let's journey inside the factory to see how every
 bottle of Coca-Cola is brought to life.
 It all starts with Coca-Cola's secret syrup.
 Surprisingly, it's not guarded by security, but arrives simply on a forklift in plastic jugs.
 This syrup is the essence of Coke, and when diluted with water in a mixing unit,
 it creates the base we all love.
 But it's not only mixed with water, after the syrup is diluted, precise amounts of
 natural or artificial sweeteners are added depending on the product.
 For instance, high fructose corn syrup is used for the classic Coca-Cola,
 while aspartame is chosen for Diet Coke and Coke Zero.
 This mixture of syrup, water, and sweetener is then blended and pumped through
 an intricate system of pipes into a succession of batch tanks in an adjoining room.
 Next, the mixture flows to the votator at the mixing station, where it meets its
 fizzy partner, CO2.
 This carbonator does the crucial job of infusing the mix with carbon dioxide,
 giving it that hiss sound when you open the drink.
 Before the bottles can be filled, they must be spotless.
 They arrive fresh from the factory, but still get a thorough rinse.
 The rinsing machine flips them upside down, flushes them with filtered water,
 then returns them upright, ready for the next step.
 Now, the bottles are labeled before any Coke touches them.
 This ensures every bottle is branded perfectly with the iconic red and
 white logo.
 This hot melt modular labeling machine works with precision.
 Swiftly affixing each label with precise alignment and accuracy on a large scale.
 But the quality checks don't stop there.
 High-tech inspection machines scans every cleaned bottle for defects like cracks
 or improper seals.
 Any bottle that doesn't pass inspection is removed from the line.
 Then comes the filling.
 The syrup mixture, kept at a precise 41 to 43 degrees, flows into the bottles.
 This careful temperature control means every bottle is filled perfectly,
 ensuring stability and preventing foaming.
 Once filled, the bottles head to the filler's reservoir.
 Air is removed and each bottle is filled with a specific amount of Coca-Cola.
 Then the caps come flying down a chute, propelled by compressed air,
 and are twisted onto the bottles.
 The capping machine ensures that each cap is sealed tightly,
 locking in the carbonation.
 But it's not over yet.
 The glass bottles get an aluminum crown that's crimped on securely
 by the crown capping machine.
 Then the bottles travel through a warmer where they're brought to room temperature
 to prevent them from sweating during packaging.
 Finally, they undergo one last inspection to catch any impurities
 before being hoisted into cases.
 Cans follow a similar journey.
 First, they're prepared and cleaned by the can cleaning system,
 ensuring they're in perfect condition.
 The syrup is mixed with water and sweeteners,
 then pumped into the filling lines.
 Cans are filled with the carefully balanced mixture,
 just like the bottles.
 Capping the cans is just as precise.
 A dedicated worker oversees the can semer,
 making sure it never runs out of caps.
 Each can is tightly sealed,
 ensuring the carbonation stays intact.
 After capping, the cans are washed and inspected
 by advanced machines.
 High-speed cameras and sensors check for misaligned seams
 or accidental spills,
 ensuring only the best cans move forward.
 Now it's time for packaging.
 Once the bottles are filled and capped,
 they're wrapped in cardboard for protection.
 The cardboard is unwound from a reel,
 cut to the correct length,
 and shaped to fit around the bottles.
 This wrap-around packaging is then secured
 with pre-folded clips,
 ensuring the bottles stay in place during transit.
 It's like tucking them in for a safe journey.
 After being wrapped in cardboard,
 the bottles are shrink-wrapped for added protection.
 The shrink wrap is applied tightly,
 providing a secure, tamper-proof seal
 that keeps the bottles safe and intact
 throughout their journey.
 The cans are carefully packed into sturdy boxes.
 Machines ensure that these boxes
 are precisely the right size to hold the cans securely,
 minimizing movement during transit.
 Think of it as a perfect puzzle,
 where every piece fits just right.
 Once the cans are packed,
 they are shrink-wrapped to secure them for shipping.
 This process involves wrapping a plastic film
 around the boxes and heating it
 to shrink the film tightly around the cans.
 Shrink-wrapping ensures that the cans
 stay firmly in place.
 The bottle cases are then sorted and stacked
 by automated systems.
 This step ensures everything is organized
 and ready for loading onto pallets.
 Efficient sorting means quicker,
 smoother deliveries to stores,
 keeping shelves stocked and ready for you.
 Just like the bottles,
 the can cases are sorted and stacked with precision.
 This careful organization helps
 in the smooth handling and shipping of large quantities.
 The sorted cases are shrink-wrapped
 to keep them stable during transport.
 This final wrap ensures everything stays put,
 reducing the risk of shifting during shipping.
 Robots then take over to organize the pallets.
 These machines stack the cases with precision,
 optimizing space and ensuring everything is secure.
 The robotic pallet organizers work efficiently
 to maximize stability
 and ensure that each pallet is perfectly balanced
 and ready for transport.
 Finally, the pallets are loaded onto trucks.
 Careful monitoring ensures everything is secure
 and the trucks are packed efficiently.
 Once loaded, they're off to distribution centers,
 bringing Coca-Cola to picnics and parties near you.
 And just like that,
 your favorite fizzy drink is on its way.
